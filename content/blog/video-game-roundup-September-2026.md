## Final Fantasy 7 Rebirth

Beat it! Kind of a silly and confusing ending, though it still had heart. I like how it played with expectations and was able to make something that felt new with them, instead of a rerun of the original game. Overall I feel like the people that don't feel this trilogy have valid points, but those that just wanted a straight remake of the original game are a bit too anchored in the past. We're in the 2020's, and games are just different now, both from a development perspective and also from a business perspective. As much as there is a want for something more true to the original, this *adaptation* of the original is true to this strange decade. We should all be happy that this isn't a more financialized product, which is what is happening to so much of games today. 

I've enjoyed Remake and Rebirth, and I think I'll be happy playing Revelations when it comes out next year. And also, let me just say that somehow Tetsuya Nomrua has become such a leading figure for game design for these games, and it's surprised me. His KH-style take on this Remake trilogy is amazing.
