## Fantasy 7 Remake

Also playing the remake! This is a weird thing - it's structured like a PS3 game (it released in 2020), is uneven in how it presents Midgar and the larger world, and the characters have a lot of moments together but say little. The battle system is fun though, and on balance it's interesting enough to stay with.
