# Video Game Roundup

Here's a view of the games I've been playing recently... ish. Lots happened these several months so I haven't had the time to write down what games I was playing because...

### WoW Classic

Got bit by the bug, and I want to do a larger writeup on my journey to 60 in TBC Anniversary. It was fun! A lot of fun. But also strange. It's hard to write my feelings for this game - it's very complicated. 

### WoW Retail

Midnight is kinda cool, but it's hard for any modern game to catch me, there's just too much overstimulation in a live environment. 

### Cult of the Lamb

Most of the way through the base game, and I really want to get into Woolhaven content. I like it! It's cute, the base buildnig stuff is cozy, and you don't really need to go crazy deep into it to get some satisfaction out of it. 

### Marathon

I continue to be the easiest mark for Cyberpunk content. However, this game is goooood. Put this on the shelf of "hits the cyberpunk mark" with Citizen Sleeper and Neuromancer pls. 

### Looking Ahead

Some more gaming, yeah. But the future is murky, muddy. Hard to see.
