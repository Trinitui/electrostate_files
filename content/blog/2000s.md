# The Shift

During the 2000's, the world and worlds of video games surged and buckled under the weight of popular culture, hardware advances, and economic dislocations. It's a decade that brought us Kingdom Hearts, Cave Story, WiiWare, World of Warcraft, and many other diverse experinces. 

This series will dive into games made from 2000 - 2010, analyzing their impact and what impacted them. I assume I'll be exploring many themes.
