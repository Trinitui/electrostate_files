Summer Series

Starting in 2023? 2024?, I started using my summers to play through a series of games. Typically, I’m busy in the spring and fall and don’t have time to indulge in hobbies. While I have a bit more time over the winter, it’s not really the best time for playing through a series for me - don’t know why! So summer it is. Something about chilling out in A/C playing through a series is very relaxing to me.

Everyone should do this, it’s fun!
