How to setup and/or maintain a home surveillance system.

  • RPi4

    • Flash SD card with HomeAssistant OS, insert into 4
    • Load it, using nmcli (with –ask) to connect to wifi
    • Should be good from there, connect and you are golden
    • Addons used:
      • MotionEye

  • Cameras

    • MotioneyeOS
      • Flash Sd Cards with most recent release
      • Connect into Home Assistant

  • Connecting over the internet
